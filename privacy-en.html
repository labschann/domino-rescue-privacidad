01. Contact and scope

This policy explains how information is handled in Domino Rescue, the Android application, and on this privacy page. For questions about the application, this policy or your rights, contact labschann@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy — Domino Rescue”.

You do not need a separate Domino Rescue account to play. The campaign and free hints work offline. Rewarded advertising and Premium purchases are optional; availability depends on the version, configuration and third-party services.

02. Information handled

  • Local progress and preferences: missions, achievements, attempts, materials, buildings, styles, language, audio, vibration, reduced motion and alternate controls are stored to save and restore your experience.
  • Age band for advertising: when preparing optional advertising, you can choose under 13, 13–17 or 18 and older. Only that band is stored locally. We do not request a date of birth or identity document. This is not an age verification process.
  • Optional local metrics: enabling them in Settings stores daily counts of sessions, starts, failures, victories, hints and commercial interactions. They are not sent to a game analytics server. Turning metrics off deletes the counts. This setting does not control technical data required by Google Play or AdMob when you use their services.
  • Advertising and consent, when enabled: Google Mobile Ads and UMP may process IP address, approximate location derived from IP, technical device information, available identifiers, advertising interactions, diagnostics and privacy choices. Actual processing depends on the declared age band, consent choices, region and Google's settings. The app declares the Android AD_ID permission to allow advertising services to use the identifier when it is available and appropriate under the applicable age, consent and configuration settings. The permission does not replace consent or guarantee that the identifier is available.
  • Purchases, when enabled: Google Play handles payment using your store account. The game's verification service receives a purchase token and a random request identifier, checks the product, purchase status, acknowledgement and refund information with Google, and returns a signed license. We do not receive your payment card details. Connections may generate technical data such as IP address, time and request outcome.
  • Promotional activation: redeeming a valid code stores a Premium access flag on this device. The code is not sent to a game server and does not become a Google Play purchase. Clearing app data removes local activation; a valid code can be redeemed again.
  • Support: if you email us, your email address and information you choose to include are used to respond. Do not send passwords, signing keys, card numbers or purchase tokens.

The game does not request contacts, microphone, camera or precise GPS location access. It does not integrate social accounts, public chat, its own remote analytics or physical activity tracking.

03. Purposes and applicable bases

Information is used to save progress, apply accessibility preferences, verify and restore Premium, prevent abuse and answer requests. Local metrics require your voluntary activation. Optional advertising helps fund the game and provides the promised hint after the ad is completed.

Where your region requires a legal basis, processing necessary to provide requested features relies on performance of the service, legal duties are addressed where applicable, and choices requiring consent are requested through the appropriate mechanisms. Security and fraud prevention are handled under bases permitted by applicable law. Selecting an age band or accepting this policy does not replace advertising consent.

04. Advertising choices

In Settings → Ad privacy, you can view the advertising status, change your age band and open Google's privacy options when available for your region. When UMP indicates that privacy options are required, the app offers access to the form. If it cannot load, you can continue playing and try again with a connection.

Ads are rewarded and voluntary. They do not appear automatically during a rescue and are not required to retry or rebuild. Basic hints are free; intermediate and advanced hints unlock after two and three failures, respectively, even when ad inventory is available. Completing one ad can bring forward an advanced hint.

If advertising is disabled in your version, the button explains this and does not request ads. When enabled, eligibility is checked before requesting ads. Changing privacy choices discards ads prepared under the previous context. Declining optional choices does not block the campaign and may limit ad type or availability.

05. Audience aged 13 and older

Domino Rescue is intended for teenagers and adults aged 13 and older. No age option is preselected and you may continue without advertising. Neither advertising nor advertising consent requests are started when age is unknown or under 13.

For users who select 13–17, the app applies conservative restricted advertising treatment, does not ask for adult consent, and disables ad personalization and remarketing for those requests. For users selecting 18 and older, the relevant privacy choices and consent requirements apply. Ad content is limited to T or lower.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided information to support or a service operated for the game, please contact us so we can assess the request. An identity document is not needed for an initial inquiry.

06. Providers and external communications

When you use the relevant features, information may be communicated to Google for ads, consent, payments, verification and fraud prevention, and to infrastructure providers hosting verification or this page. Information may also be disclosed in response to a valid legal duty or when necessary to protect users' security and rights.

Google handles information under its terms and policies. See the Google Privacy Policy, Google advertising information and advertising SDK data categories. This integration does not add other advertising mediation networks.

We do not trade local save files or local metrics. Advertising can involve communications that some laws define as “sharing”; applicable choices are managed through available advertising controls. Information may be processed outside your country depending on providers' infrastructure and applicable safeguards.

07. Retention and deletion

  • Progress and preferences: remain on your device until you clear app data or uninstall. Android may retain or restore a backup of progress, depending on device and account settings.
  • Local counts: entries older than 90 days are removed when new events are recorded. Turning metrics off immediately deletes the counts.
  • Age band and commerce preferences: are stored locally and excluded by the app's backup and transfer rules. You can change your age band through Ad privacy or remove local information by clearing app data.
  • Premium license: is stored in the app's no-backup area and lasts up to seven days without renewed verification. Online verification renews access without another purchase. Google Play retains the purchase record under its own rules.
  • Verification: the service code does not maintain a token database or write tokens to its logs. A cryptographic fingerprint is held temporarily for rate limiting. Hosting infrastructure may retain technical access records under its configuration and security needs.
  • Support: messages are retained while necessary to respond, document the outcome or comply with applicable duties. You can request deletion where applicable.

To remove an Android backup or information held by Google, also use your account controls or contact that provider. Deleting the app does not automatically cancel or refund a Google Play purchase.

08. Your controls and rights

You can read this policy in Settings → Privacy policy, use advertising choices, turn off metrics or clear local information through Android settings. There is no separate Domino Rescue account to delete.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability and withdrawal of consent, and the right to complain to a competent authority. For information under our control, email labschann@gmail.com. Identify the app and describe the request; we will seek only proportionate information needed to verify it. We cannot identify or change a local file we cannot access.

For information managed directly by Google, use its account controls and privacy channels. Withdrawing consent does not affect earlier lawful processing. Requests will be handled within applicable deadlines; information may need to be retained where a legal duty applies.

09. Security

Purchase services use HTTPS connections. Premium licenses are checked using cryptographic signatures, and private verification credentials are not included in the application. Sensitive logging is limited. No storage or transmission method is completely secure; keep the game updated and protect your device and store account.

10. This website

This page is informational. It contains no forms, advertising, tracking pixels or analytics added by Domino Rescue, and it requires no game account. Your browser and hosting infrastructure process technical information necessary to serve and protect the page. It is hosted on GitHub Pages: GitHub logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement. External links lead to services that apply their own policies.
